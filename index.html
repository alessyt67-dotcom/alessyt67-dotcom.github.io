The Dark Orbit

Eerie sci-fi mysteries, told in parts.

It's the 2140s. Humanity lives in thin-walled outposts across the solar system: a colony on Mars, a station under the ice of Europa, mining crews in the asteroid belt. And something very old is waking up.

Every story follows one person who notices that something is wrong. Each part is about a minute long, made for vertical video, and ends on a cliffhanger. All stories share one universe, and small clues connect them.

The Forty-First Heartbeat

Dr. Lena Ortiz is the doctor of Ares Colony on Mars. Every night her scanner counts forty sleeping heartbeats. One night it counts forty-one. A sci-fi mystery in 5 parts.

The Dark Orbit Publisher

The Dark Orbit Publisher is the desktop app the studio uses to publish its finished story parts to TikTok. After a part has been reviewed, the creator opens it in the Publisher and:

  1. signs in with TikTok (TikTok Login Kit) and sees which TikTok account the video will go to;
  2. watches a preview of the video and edits its caption;
  3. chooses who can see the video and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed;
  4. discloses promotional or branded content when it applies, and labels the video as AI-generated;
  5. agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and presses Post. The video is uploaded with TikTok's Content Posting API and appears on the creator's profile once TikTok has processed it.

The Publisher only posts videos the creator has chosen and confirmed. It doesn't read or collect data about other TikTok users. See the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
