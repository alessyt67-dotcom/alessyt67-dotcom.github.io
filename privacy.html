Privacy Policy

The Dark Orbit Publisher. Last updated: November 18, 2026

The Dark Orbit Publisher ("the app") is the desktop app The Dark Orbit uses to publish its own videos to its TikTok account. This page explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with TikTok, TikTok gives the app an access token for the permissions you approve:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account a video will be posted to;
  • video.publish: posting a video you have chosen to your TikTok profile. Before each post the app also asks TikTok which privacy options and interaction settings your account allows, and shows them to you.

The app uploads only the video file and the settings you choose for it (caption, who can see it, comments, duets, stitches, content disclosure, AI-generated label).

Where data is kept

The access token is stored only on the computer where the app runs. It is sent only to TikTok's own API (open.tiktokapis.com). The app has no server of its own and stores nothing online.

What the app does not do

It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers. It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and it uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. This website uses no cookies.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or "Manage app permissions"). Signing out in the app, or deleting its token file, removes all TikTok data the app holds.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always on this page, with its date at the top.
